What Vyu is

Expense tracking shouldn't feel like accounting.

The problem

Most expense apps want fifteen dollars a month, then bury the answer under ads, charts and jargon you never asked for. You open them twice and never again.

Who it's for

People who just want a clear view of their money. Vyu works in the background, stays out of your way, and only shows what actually matters.

How it works

Three steps, and two of them aren't yours.

01

Pay as usual

Tap with Apple Pay, scan the receipt, say it out loud, or paste what you copied. Whatever is quickest.

02

Vyu catches it

Merchant, amount and category land on your lock screen, already saved. Swipe down only if something needs changing.

03

See what matters

One screen tells you where the month went. No spreadsheets, no math.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Know what's left, not just what's gone

Split your income between budgets and savings, then let Vyu do the arithmetic: what's left this month, how much a day keeps you on track, and a nudge on your lock screen well before you run out.

Your month at a glance

A calendar of your spending day by day, and a donut that shows where it goes. Search your history by month or by financial year.

Every expense, receipt included

Each expense opens as a paper ticket: merchant, amount, category, payment method, and the receipt itself still attached. Tap it to read the whole thing at full size.

Always within reach

Wherever you already are.

On your home screen

See today, this week and this month at a glance, and tap the microphone to say an expense out loud, without opening the app.

Pay with your Apple Watch

Payments you make with Apple Watch are logged the same way as on your iPhone, and you can accept or undo them right from your wrist.

Vyu does not have its own Apple Watch app.

Privacy

Your money is nobody else's business.

Where your expenses live

Vyu has no servers of its own where your expenses are kept. Everything lives on your iPhone and, if you want, in your own iCloud backup. Purchases are handled by Apple.

What Vyu never asks for

No account, no bank logins, no read access to your accounts. Vyu only ever sees what you hand it: a tap, a receipt, an email, a sentence.

Questions

Before you ask.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. No bank logins, no read access to your accounts. Vyu only sees what you show it: an Apple Pay tap, a receipt, an email, a sentence you say out loud.

What happens when I change phone?

Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup and your expenses come across with it. You can also export everything to CSV first and keep your own copy.

Are there ads?

None, on either plan. Vyu is paid for by the people who choose Pro, not by advertisers.

Does it need an internet connection?

Your expenses are on your phone, so browsing, adding and editing them works without a connection. Some of the automatic capture depends on things that arrive online, such as email receipts and pasted links.

When are Duo and Travel coming?

They are planned for v2 and we are not putting a date on them. They will be included in Pro at no extra cost.

Is there an Android version?

Not for now. Vyu is built around things only an iPhone does — Apple Pay, Shortcuts, widgets — so an Android version would be a different app, not a port.
